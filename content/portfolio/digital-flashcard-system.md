## Overview

This project applied the science of spaced repetition to a simple, distraction-free flashcard interface. Rather than showing cards randomly, the system schedules reviews based on how confidently a student rated their last attempt — surfacing difficult cards more often and familiar ones less frequently.

## Key Features

- Confidence-based scheduling using a simplified SM-2 spaced repetition algorithm
- Support for text, image, and audio card types
- Progress dashboard showing retention rates by topic
- Offline-first design so students can study without a reliable internet connection

## Why It Matters

Rote memorisation has a reputation problem — and rightfully so when done poorly. This tool aims to rehabilitate flashcard learning by making it smarter, not harder. Students who used it in a six-week pilot reported feeling more confident going into assessments.

## Audience

Secondary and post-secondary students preparing for high-stakes exams, and educators looking to recommend evidence-based study tools.
