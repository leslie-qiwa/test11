## Overview

Good lesson planning is invisible to students and invaluable to teachers. This library gives educators a head start with twenty professionally designed templates, each built around a specific pedagogical model and ready to adapt for any subject or year group.

## Templates Included

- Direct instruction and modelled learning
- Inquiry-based and discovery learning
- Flipped classroom structures
- Project-based learning (PBL) multi-week arcs
- Socratic seminar and discussion-based formats
- Differentiated instruction frameworks for mixed-ability groups

## Design Principles

Each template was designed with three priorities: clarity for the teacher planning the lesson, flexibility for different subjects and year levels, and usefulness as a reflection document after the lesson is taught.

## Format

Available in Google Docs, Microsoft Word, and PDF. All templates include inline guidance notes explaining the purpose of each section and suggestions for adapting them to different contexts.
