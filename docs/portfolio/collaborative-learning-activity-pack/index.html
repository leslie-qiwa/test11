Collaborative Learning Activity Pack — Group Thinking Toolkit

Overview

Designed for secondary school classrooms, this activity pack gives teachers a ready-to-run toolkit for structured collaborative learning. It includes facilitated discussion cards, role-assignment frameworks, and reflection prompts that guide students through group tasks without the usual chaos.

What’s Included

  • 12 discussion card sets covering STEM, humanities, and creative subjects
  • A facilitator guide with timing suggestions and differentiation tips
  • Digital versions compatible with Google Slides and Microsoft PowerPoint
  • A student self-assessment rubric aligned to collaborative skills frameworks

The Thinking Behind It

Most group work fails not because students can’t collaborate, but because the structure isn’t there to support it. This pack removes the ambiguity — every student knows their role, every group knows the goal, and the teacher has room to observe rather than manage.

Impact

Piloted with three year groups across two schools. Teacher feedback highlighted a measurable improvement in student engagement and a reduction in off-task behaviour during group sessions.
